Photo privacy guide

How to check what metadata is in a photo

Image metadata is information stored alongside the image data. Depending on the device, file format, and settings, a photo may include a capture date, camera or phone model, exposure settings, GPS coordinates, editing software, or a small embedded preview.

Check photo details on your device

  • Windows: In File Explorer, right-click the image, select Properties, then open the Details tab.
  • macOS: Open the photo in Preview and use Tools → Show Inspector to review available image information.
  • iPhone or iPad: Open the photo in Photos and tap the info button or swipe up to view the details available there.
  • Android: Open the photo in your gallery or Google Photos and look for Details or Info. The available fields vary by app and device.

These built-in views may not show every metadata field or embedded thumbnail. Treat them as a useful check, not a guarantee that a file contains no other metadata.
