Remove metadata from JPG and JPEG photos

Use this free, private JPEG EXIF remover to remove EXIF data online by creating a clean copy in your browser. The original EXIF, GPS, XMP, and embedded thumbnail metadata is not carried over, and your image is not uploaded.

JPEG photos can contain when and where a picture was taken, camera make and model, lens and exposure settings, software details, and location coordinates. Remove or delete EXIF data before sharing a photo when you don’t want to include those details.

Quality note: JPEG images must be re-encoded to remove metadata this way. That can slightly change image quality; choose a higher quality setting in the tool to limit the difference. Your original stays unchanged.

How to strip EXIF data from a JPEG

  • Open the image metadata remover and select a JPG or JPEG file.
  • Adjust output quality if you want, then choose “Remove metadata.”
  • Save the new copy. The original image on your device is not modified.

Private JPEG EXIF removal, without upload

Processing runs in your browser using the image APIs built into the browser. The tool does not send your photo to an image-processing service. JPEG EXIF removal works when your browser can decode and export JPEG images.
