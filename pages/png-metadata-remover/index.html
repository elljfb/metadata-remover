Remove metadata from PNG images

This PNG metadata remover redraws your image in your browser and creates a new PNG copy. Embedded text, editing details, location fields, and other metadata from the original are not copied into the output.

PNG is commonly used for screenshots, illustrations, and images with transparency. Browser PNG output is lossless, and the transparent areas are retained. The output may include basic encoder information, but not the original photo metadata.

Good to know: Metadata removal does not hide identifying details that are visible in the image itself, such as a name, address, or face.
Remove metadata from a PNG

Clean a PNG image in three steps

  • Select a PNG image from your device.
  • Choose “Remove metadata.” PNG output is lossless and does not use the quality slider.
  • Download the cleaned PNG. Your original remains unchanged.

PNG metadata removal in your browser

The PNG is handled locally by your browser; no image-processing server is involved.
